01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites can place on your device when you visit them. They help websites remember information about your visit and can make the browsing experience more convenient.

Cookies may be stored temporarily during your visit or remain on your device for a specified period depending on their purpose.

02

How We Use Cookies

Safe Step may use cookies and similar technologies to support the operation of our website and improve the experience of visitors.

  • Remembering selected website preferences.
  • Supporting website functionality.
  • Understanding how visitors interact with the website.
  • Improving website performance and usability.
03

Types of Cookies

Cookies can generally be categorized according to their purpose and how they are used by a website.

The categories that may be relevant to the Safe Step website include essential cookies, preference cookies, analytics cookies, and cookies provided by third-party services.

04

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies may be necessary for certain website features to operate correctly. They can support basic functionality, navigation, security, and user-selected settings.

Because these cookies may support core website functionality, disabling them could affect certain features of the website.

05

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies may allow the website to remember choices made during your visit.

These may include settings such as language preferences, display preferences, or other options that help provide a more personalized browsing experience.

06

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies may be used to understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are visited and how users navigate between different areas.

This information can help identify opportunities to improve website performance, content, accessibility, and usability.

07

Third-Party Cookies

Some features or services used on the website may be provided by third parties. These providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies.

Third-party cookies are controlled by the respective third party and may be subject to that provider's own privacy and cookie policies.

08

Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to manage or delete cookies through their settings.

You may be able to configure your browser to block cookies, notify you when cookies are being stored, or delete existing cookies.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or appearance of some parts of the website.

09

Cookie Duration

Cookies may be either session cookies or persistent cookies.

  • Session cookies are generally removed when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies may remain on your device until they expire or are deleted.

The duration of a cookie may depend on its purpose and the service that places it.

10

Changes to This Policy

Safe Step may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, technologies used, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date where appropriate.
